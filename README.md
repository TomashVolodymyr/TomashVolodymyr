### Telegram Ordering Service

A customer browses the catalog, fills a cart and pays in advance without leaving Telegram; the owner accepts or rejects the order in an admin bot, and a rejected order is refunded automatically. Payments run through a provider-agnostic layer, and every client gets an isolated deployment built and shipped by CI.

- **Case study:** [telegram-ordering-case-study](https://github.com/TomashVolodymyr/telegram-ordering-case-study)

### ReplyFlow

A multi-tenant SaaS that reads Instagram conversations, queries the product catalog, detects purchase intent and creates orders through a Claude Tool Use agent.

- **Live:** https://reply-flow.com
- **Case study:** [replyflow-case-study](https://github.com/TomashVolodymyr/replyflow-case-study)

### Strunki

A Telegram bot with onboarding, calorie and macro calculations, menu planning, progress tracking, scheduled jobs and subscriptions through Telegram Stars.

- **Live:** https://t.me/strunki_menu_bot
- **Case study:** [strunki-case-study](https://github.com/TomashVolodymyr/strunki-case-study)
